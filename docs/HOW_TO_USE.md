# How to Use the AI Content Workflow System

## Welcome! 👋

This guide will show you exactly how to use our AI-powered content management system. You don't need any technical experience - just follow these simple steps!

## What This System Does

Our system helps you:
- **Create marketing campaigns** (like for a product launch or event)
- **Generate content automatically** using AI (headlines, descriptions, etc.)
- **Translate content** to different languages 
- **Review and approve** AI-generated content
- **Analyze content** for tone, keywords, and sentiment

---

## Example 1: Creating Your First Marketing Campaign 🚀

**Scenario**: You want to launch a new fitness app and need marketing content.

### Step 1: Create a Campaign
1. Go to the **Campaigns** page (you'll see this when you first open the app)
2. Click the blue **"Create Campaign"** button
3. Fill out the form:
   - **Campaign Name**: "Fitness App Launch"
   - **Description**: "Marketing campaign for our new fitness tracking app"
   - **Default Language**: English
   - **Target Languages**: Spanish, French (optional - for translations later)
4. Click **"Create Campaign"**

✅ **Result**: You now have a campaign! You'll see it listed on the campaigns page.

### Step 2: Add Content to Your Campaign
1. Click on your "Fitness App Launch" campaign to open it
2. Click the green **"Add Content"** button
3. Fill out the form:
   - **Content Type**: Choose "Headline"
   - **Original Content**: "Get fit with our revolutionary new app"
   - **Language**: English
4. Click **"Create Content"**

✅ **Result**: You'll see a content card with your headline. It shows status as "draft".

### Step 3: Generate AI Content
1. On your content card, click the blue **"AI Generate"** button
2. Wait a few seconds (you'll see "Generating..." on the button)
3. The page will refresh automatically

✅ **Result**: Your content now shows "ai generated" status and displays AI-improved versions of your headline!

### Step 4: Review the AI Content
1. Click the orange **"Submit for Review"** button on your content card
2. Then click the **"Review Content"** button that appears
3. In the review modal, you'll see:
   - Your original content on the left
   - AI-generated content on the right
4. Choose one of the options:
   - **Green "Approve"** - Content is good to use
   - **Yellow "Needs Revision"** - Content needs changes
   - **Red "Reject"** - Content is not suitable

✅ **Result**: Your content is now approved and ready to use!

## Example 2: International Product Launch 🌍

**Scenario**: You're launching a cooking app globally and need content in multiple languages.

### Step 1: Create an International Campaign
1. Click **"Create Campaign"**
2. Fill out:
   - **Campaign Name**: "Global Cooking App Launch"
   - **Default Language**: English
   - **Target Languages**: Spanish, French, German, Italian
3. Click **"Create Campaign"**

### Step 2: Create Multiple Content Types
1. Click on your campaign to open it
2. Add different types of content by clicking **"Add Content"** multiple times:
   
   **First Content Piece**:
   - **Type**: Headline
   - **Content**: "Master cooking with AI-powered recipes"
   
   **Second Content Piece**:
   - **Type**: Description  
   - **Content**: "Our smart cooking app learns your preferences and suggests personalized recipes"
   
   **Third Content Piece**:
   - **Type**: Call to Action
   - **Content**: "Download now and start cooking like a chef!"

### Step 3: Generate AI Content for All
1. For each content card, click **"AI Generate"**
2. Wait for each to process (you'll see improved versions)

### Step 4: Translate Everything
1. Use the **"Translate All Targets"** button at the top of the campaign
2. This will automatically translate all your content to Spanish, French, German, and Italian
3. Wait for the translations to complete

✅ **Result**: You now have professional marketing content in 5 languages!

### Step 5: Review by Language
1. In the review modal, you can switch between languages using the dropdown
2. Review and approve content for each language separately

## Example 3: Content Analysis and Optimization 📊

**Scenario**: You want to analyze your content to make sure it has the right tone and keywords.

### Step 1: Create Content for Analysis
1. Create a new campaign: "Brand Voice Analysis"
2. Add content with different tones:
   
   **Professional Content**:
   - **Type**: Body Content
   - **Content**: "Our enterprise solution delivers robust performance and scalable architecture for business operations"
   
   **Casual Content**:
   - **Type**: Social Media Post
   - **Content**: "Hey fitness lovers! Ready to crush your goals? Our app is here to help you stay motivated!"
   
   **Enthusiastic Content**:
   - **Type**: Headline
   - **Content**: "Amazing results await! Transform your life with this incredible breakthrough!"

### Step 2: Analyze Each Content Piece
1. On each content card, click the purple **"Analyze"** button
2. Wait a moment (you'll see "Analyzing...")
3. The analysis results will appear below each content card

✅ **What you'll see**:
- **Keywords**: Important words found in your content
- **Tone**: Professional, casual, enthusiastic, etc.
- **Sentiment**: Positive, negative, or neutral (with confidence percentage)
- **Confidence**: How sure the AI is about the analysis

### Step 3: Use Filters to Find Content
1. At the top of your campaign, you'll see filter options:
   - **Status Filter**: Find draft, approved, or rejected content
   - **Tone Filter**: Find content with specific tones (professional, casual, etc.)
   - **Sentiment Filter**: Find positive, negative, or neutral content
   - **Keywords**: Search for specific words in your content

2. Try filtering by tone "enthusiastic" to see only upbeat content
3. Try searching for the keyword "amazing" to find content with that word

✅ **Result**: You can now easily organize and find content based on its characteristics!

## Quick Reference: What Each Button Does

| Button | What it Does |
|--------|-------------|
| 🔵 **AI Generate** | Creates improved versions of your content using AI |
| 🟢 **Translate** | Translates content to Spanish (or click "Translate All Targets" for all languages) |
| 🟣 **Analyze** | Examines your content for keywords, tone, and sentiment |
| 🟠 **Submit for Review** | Moves content from draft to review status |
| 🟠 **Review Content** | Opens the review interface to approve/reject content |
| 🔵 **Language Status** | Shows review status for each language |

---

## Content Status Explained

Your content moves through these stages:

1. **Draft** (Gray) - Just created, not processed yet
2. **AI Generated** (Blue) - AI has created improved versions  
3. **Under Review** (Yellow) - Waiting for human approval
4. **Approved** (Green) - Ready to use!
5. **Rejected** (Red) - Needs to be redone

## Tips for Success ✨

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with one campaign and a few pieces of content
2. **Use Descriptive Names**: Give your campaigns clear names so you can find them later
3. **Review AI Suggestions**: Always check AI-generated content before approving
4. **Use Analysis**: The analyze feature helps ensure consistent brand voice
5. **Plan for Multiple Languages**: Set up target languages when creating campaigns
6. **Filter and Search**: Use the filters to quickly find specific content

---

## Need Help?

- **Can't find a button?** Make sure you've completed the previous step (e.g., you need to generate AI content before you can submit for review)
- **Content not loading?** Try refreshing the page
- **Translations not working?** Make sure you set target languages when creating your campaign
